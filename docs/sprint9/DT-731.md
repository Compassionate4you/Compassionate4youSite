# DT-731 — Home Health screenshot content and layout migration

Sprint: 9

## Changes

- Our Home Health Services uses HeaderCardsSection with eight service names in screenshot order and heart icons. Descriptions are omitted to match the screenshot content; the section has one heading.
- Home Health Advantages uses HeaderCardsSection with `headerPosition='right'`: heading on the right, seven cards on the left at desktop widths. The existing responsive layout places the heading above the cards on smaller screens. All seven screenshot statements remain body text in their original order, with the component's default heart icons and numbering.
- Specialty Services uses HeaderCardsSection with all ten screenshot specialty names in order and heart icons.
- Content comes directly from existing translation keys: `homeHealth.servicesHeading`, `homeHealth.services`, and `homeHealthPage` advantages/specialties keys. No CMS records, seeds, or translations are modified.
- The Home Health adapter replaces presentation for `sec-hh-services`, `sec-hh-advantages`, and `sec-hh-specialties`, retaining their configured page positions. Other sections retain the dynamic renderer and theme forwarding.
- Both `homeHealth` and the `home-health` URL alias select the adapter. The existing Hospice adapter remains in use for Hospice.
- All three sections use the existing HeaderCardsSection.css and shared foundation styles. The Home Health adapter no longer imports HeaderStackSection. No legacy card-section/card-grid markup is emitted. Services and specialties keep left-hand headings; no backgrounds or subtitles are added. Shared stylesheets are unchanged.
